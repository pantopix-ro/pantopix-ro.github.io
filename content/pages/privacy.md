PANTOPIX RO SRL ("we") runs pantopix.ro. This page explains what personal data we process when you visit the site or write to us, and why.

## Who is responsible

PANTOPIX RO SRL, Str. Înfrățirii 30, Cluj-Napoca, Romania. CUI 51113624. Email: salut@pantopix.com. We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer under Article 37 GDPR.

## What we collect

**When you visit the site.** The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every request for security, under its own privacy statement. We set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are served from our own domain.

**When you request the audit or write to us.** The audit form opens an email in your own mail program, addressed to salut@pantopix.com. We receive what you send: usually your name, work email, company, role and a description of your problem. We use it to answer you and to prepare the audit. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, steps before a contract, and Article 6(1)(f), our interest in answering business enquiries.

**When you book a call.** Bookings run on Cal.com, which processes your name, email and the time you pick under its own privacy policy.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 24 months after the last contact, unless they lead to a contract. Then we keep them for as long as accounting and tax law require.

## Who we share it with

With PANTOPIX GmbH & Co. KG in Germany when a project involves both companies, and with our email provider. We don't sell personal data. We don't transfer it outside the EEA except through services that use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, and you can object to processing. Write to salut@pantopix.com. You can also complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro), or to the authority where you live.

## Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of the page.
