## What we do

We build the product your customers use: the web app, the mobile app and the backend behind them. A senior developer owns the code from the first commit. Design and DevOps join for the days you need them.

## Typical projects

- An MVP for a seed-stage startup, live in 8 to 12 weeks.
- A customer portal or app for a manufacturer, next to the systems it already runs.
- A cross-platform mobile app in React Native, with offline mode, QR scanning and push notifications.

## How it starts

With the free audit, or a 30-minute call. Then a discovery week, and a demo every Friday after that.
