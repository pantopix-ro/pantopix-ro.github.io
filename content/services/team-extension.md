## What we do

We propose engineers by skill and time zone, and you interview them. They join your standups, take your tickets and go through your code review, as members of your team. We meet once a month to decide whether to scale up or down.

## How it works

- Profiles within 5 working days.
- A first merged pull request in week one.
- One month's notice to scale.
- When your own hires arrive, they take over our tickets. We stay for the overlap, then step out.

## How it starts

A 30-minute call about the role, the stack and the start date.
