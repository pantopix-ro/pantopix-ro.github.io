## What we do

We build the parts users never see and always notice when they break. APIs, integrations with your ERP or CRM, event pipelines, background jobs and IoT backends that take readings from thousands of devices.

## Typical projects

- An analytics platform that ingests 20,000 events per second and shows them on live dashboards.
- One API in front of several legacy systems, so your apps talk to one place.
- An IoT backend: device registry, readings over MQTT, alerts and a dashboard.

## How it starts

We read your current architecture and your load numbers first. Then we agree on the one bottleneck to fix before anything else.
