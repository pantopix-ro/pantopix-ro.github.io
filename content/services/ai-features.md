## What we do

We pick one task the AI should take over, build it into your product behind a feature flag and test it on 100 to 300 real cases before it ships. You get accuracy, cost per request and a list of how it fails, in writing.

## Typical projects

- A support assistant that answers from your own help center and hands over to a person when it isn't sure.
- An agent that fills forms or tickets from emails, with human review where mistakes cost money.
- Search over your own documents, hosted in the EU.

## How it starts

A one-page brief: the use case, who uses it and one success metric. Then a data check and a first working version in about two weeks.
