## What we do

We measure first: how often you deploy, how long a change takes to reach users, how many releases fail and how fast you recover. Then we stabilise the product, fix the essentials and coach your team. After that we build features again, and measure the same numbers a second time.

## The steps

1. Measure the baseline.
2. Stabilise: alerts, a rollback path, the top crashes fixed.
3. Fix the essentials: a CI/CD pipeline, tests on the critical paths, upgrades.
4. Ways of working: code review rules, a definition of done, a sprint rhythm.
5. Build features on a stable base.
6. Measure again, with a before and after report you can show your board.
